# NICK'S PREFERENCES
> Extracted from forensic analysis of 15 posted clips (Feb 2026)

## Topics That Perform Well
1. **Israel/Palestine History & Geography** - License plates, borders, Bethlehem, maps
2. **Islamic Theology vs. Practice** - Muhammad's actions vs Quran, polygamy, hadith
3. **Definitions of Genocide/Apartheid** - Legal definitions vs emotional claims
4. **Jewish History** - Judea etymology, Talmud, biblical references
5. **Western vs. Non-Western Morality** - Consent, human rights, cultural relativism

## Hooks That Work (Ranked)
1. **The Trap Question** - Nick asks a seemingly simple, specific question (Socratic method)
   - "Can you tell me what color the license plates are?"
   - "Where is Bethlehem?"
   - "When was Hamas created?"
2. **The Knowledge Check** - Exposing guest doesn't know basics about their own position
3. **The "Wait, hold on" Interruption** - Nick stops guest mid-claim to address something
4. **The Direct Roast/Insult** - Nick delivers a savage line directly

## What Nick Rejects
- Moments where Nick looks rattled or loses composure
- Amicable agreement on mundane topics
- Complex theological discussions with no clear winner
- Clips where the guest makes a strong unrebutted point
- Inside jokes or stream-context-dependent moments
- Technical difficulties, dead air, cross-talk without resolution

## Nick's Winning Formula
- **60% Questioner** - Nick controls by asking, not telling
- **20% Educator** - Nick teaches/explains with authority
- **10% Attacker** - Direct confrontation
- **5% Reactor / 5% Defender**
- Nick speaks FIRST in 75% of clips, speaks LAST in 90%
- Average 6 back-and-forth turns per clip
- Nick talks 65% of the time
- Nick ALWAYS wins in posted clips (100%)

## Clip Types (Priority Order)
1. **GOTCHA** - Exposing contradiction or ignorance (most common)
2. **DEBUNK** - Correcting false history with facts
3. **CONFRONTATION** - Guest runs away, insults, or melts down
4. **EDUCATION** - Etymology/history lesson delivered with authority
5. **HUMOR** - Mocking guest's logic, witty comeback

## Emotional Arc Pattern
Most common: **Curiosity → Trap → Victory/Amusement**
- Nick starts calm, curious, asking questions
- Guest commits to a position or reveals ignorance
- Nick delivers the reveal with smug satisfaction
- Peak emotions: Smug satisfaction, disbelief at guest ignorance

## Clip Duration
- Minimum: 25 seconds
- Maximum: 180 seconds
- **Sweet spot: 55-90 seconds**
- Average: 68 seconds
- Median: 60 seconds

## Structure Timing
- Setup (context before peak): 5-20 seconds (avg 12)
- Peak (the clip-worthy moment): 30-50 seconds (avg 40)
- Resolution (aftermath): 2-10 seconds (avg 5)

1. TikTok (30-60s, hook in first 1 second)
2. YouTube Shorts (up to 90s)
3. Instagram Reels (30-60s)
4. X/Twitter (controversial takes, <60s)
